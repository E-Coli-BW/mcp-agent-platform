# Refund Status

## Pending or missing refunds

A refund can take several business days to appear on the original payment
method. Customers should check the payment statement and the refund reference
provided by support before submitting a duplicate request. A refund sent to an
expired or cancelled card may require the card issuer to route the funds.

## What support can verify

Support can verify whether a refund was created, its amount, and its reference
number. Support cannot promise a bank's posting time. If the refund is still
missing after the stated processing window, provide the order and refund
reference for investigation.

Source: https://support.stripe.com/questions/refunds-faq?locale=en-GB
