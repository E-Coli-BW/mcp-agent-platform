# Payment Disputes

## Unrecognized payment

First contact the merchant and ask them to identify or refund the payment. If
the merchant is unresponsive or the resolution is unsatisfactory, contact the
card issuer to ask about a dispute or chargeback. The issuer, not the payment
processor, decides the dispute outcome.

## Evidence and timing

A dispute is different from an ordinary refund request. Customers should keep
the receipt, merchant correspondence, delivery evidence, and transaction
details. Do not claim that opening a dispute guarantees an immediate refund.
