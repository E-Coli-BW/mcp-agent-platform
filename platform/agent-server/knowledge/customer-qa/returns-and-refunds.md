# Returns, refunds, and online orders

Source: FTC Consumer Advice, Online Shopping and Solving Problems With a
Business. Canonical URLs are listed in `README.md`.

The seller's own return policy controls important details such as whether an
item can be returned, the return deadline, who pays return shipping, and
whether a restocking fee applies. Customers should check the seller's website,
receipt, product packaging, warranty, or other purchase documents before
returning an item.

For an item that was never delivered, the customer should contact the seller
promptly. If the seller has not shipped within the promised timeframe, the
customer may be able to cancel the order. Credit-card disputes have separate
deadlines and requirements, so the customer should contact the card issuer and
keep relevant records.

This public guidance does not determine a particular merchant's refund
eligibility. A support agent must not promise a refund without the merchant's
policy and order-specific evidence.
